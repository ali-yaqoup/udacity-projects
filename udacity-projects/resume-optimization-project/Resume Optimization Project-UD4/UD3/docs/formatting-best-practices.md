# Resume Formatting Best Practices

## Professional Resume Standards

### Font Guidelines
- **Primary fonts**: Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, Georgia
- **Font size**: 10-12pt for body text, 14-16pt for headers
- **Consistency**: Use same font family throughout
- **Readability**: Avoid decorative or script fonts

### Layout Principles
- **Single column** format for ATS compatibility
- **Left alignment** for all text
- **Adequate white space** between sections
- **Consistent margins** (0.5-1 inch on all sides)
- **Logical flow** from most to least important information

## Section Structure and Order

### Standard Resume Order
1. **Contact Information** (top of page)
2. **Professional Summary** (2-4 sentences)
3. **Experience** (reverse chronological order)
4. **Education** (most recent first)
5. **Skills** (categorized by type)
6. **Optional Sections**: Certifications, Projects, Awards

### Section Headers
- **Clear, standard labels**: "Professional Experience", "Education", "Skills"
- **Consistent formatting**: Bold, larger font size
- **Left alignment** with section content
- **White space** before and after each section

## Content Formatting Rules

### Bullet Points
- **Start with action verbs**: Managed, Developed, Led, Created
- **Quantify achievements**: "Increased sales by 25%", "Managed team of 10"
- **Consistent punctuation**: Periods at end of complete sentences
- **Length**: 1-2 lines maximum per bullet point
- **Parallel structure**: Similar grammatical structure throughout

### Date Formatting
- **Consistent format**: "Month Year - Month Year" or "2020 - Present"
- **Right alignment** for dates in experience section
- **No gaps** in employment timeline
- **Current position**: "Present" instead of current date

### Contact Information Layout
```
John Smith
(123) 456-7890 | john.smith@email.com | LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/johnsmith
City, State | Portfolio: johnsmith.com
```

## Visual Hierarchy

### Emphasis Techniques
- **Bold**: Job titles, company names, section headers
- **Italics**: Degree names, publications, foreign languages
- **Underline**: Avoid (can confuse ATS)
- **ALL CAPS**: Avoid except for acronyms

### Spacing Guidelines
- **Line spacing**: 1.0-1.15 between lines
- **Section spacing**: 6-12 points between sections
- **Paragraph spacing**: Consistent throughout
- **White space**: 20-30% of page should be white space

## Length and Page Management

### Optimal Length
- **Entry-level**: 1 page maximum
- **Mid-career**: 1-2 pages maximum
- **Senior-level**: 2 pages maximum (10+ years experience)
- **Academic/CV**: May be longer with publications

### Page Management
- **Avoid page breaks** in middle of sections
- **Balance content** across pages
- **Include name and page number** on second page
- **Ensure critical information** on first page

## Professional Presentation

### Color Usage
- **Primary text**: Black (#000000)
- **Background**: White (#FFFFFF)
- **Accent color**: Dark blue or gray (minimal use)
- **Avoid**: Bright colors, gradients, patterns

### Alignment and Consistency
- **Left alignment** for all body text
- **Consistent indentation** for bullet points
- **Uniform spacing** throughout document
- **Parallel formatting** for similar content

## File Format and Naming

### File Format Guidelines
- **Preferred**: PDF (preserves formatting)
- **Alternative**: .docx (for ATS systems)
- **Avoid**: .pages, .odt, or proprietary formats
- **File size**: Under 2MB for email submissions

### File Naming Convention
- **Professional format**: "FirstName_LastName_Resume.pdf"
- **Include job title**: "John_Smith_Software_Engineer_Resume.pdf"
- **Avoid spaces**: Use underscores or hyphens
- **Date version**: "John_Smith_Resume_2024.pdf"

## Proofreading and Quality Control

### Content Review Checklist
- [ ] **Spelling and grammar** error-free
- [ ] **Consistent formatting** throughout
- [ ] **Contact information** accurate and complete
- [ ] **Dates and timelines** correct
- [ ] **Quantified achievements** included

### Visual Inspection
- [ ] **Balanced layout** with adequate white space
- [ ] **Readable font size** and type
- [ ] **Consistent alignment** and spacing
- [ ] **Professional appearance** overall
- [ ] **Print-friendly** formatting

## Industry-Specific Considerations

### Creative Fields
- **Portfolio link** prominently displayed
- **Project descriptions** with visual outcomes
- **Software proficiency** highlighted
- **Awards and recognition** included

### Technical Fields
- **Technical skills** section prominent
- **Certifications** clearly listed
- **Project details** with technologies used
- **Quantifiable results** emphasized

### Executive Level
- **Executive summary** instead of objective
- **Leadership achievements** highlighted
- **Board positions** and affiliations
- **Industry recognition** and speaking engagements

## Mobile and Digital Optimization

### Mobile Viewing
- **Readable on small screens**
- **Single column format**
- **Large enough font size**
- **Minimal scrolling required**

### Email Submission
- **Professional email address**
- **Appropriate subject line**
- **Brief email body**
- **PDF attachment** with proper naming

## Common Formatting Mistakes to Avoid

### ATS Issues
- **Tables and columns** that confuse parsing
- **Headers and footers** with important information
- **Graphics and images** that can't be read
- **Unusual fonts** or special characters

### Professional Appearance
- **Inconsistent formatting** throughout
- **Too much text** or dense paragraphs
- **Poor font choices** or sizes
- **Inadequate white space**
- **Typos and grammatical errors**
