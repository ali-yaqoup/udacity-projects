# Resume Optimization Project Requirements

## Evaluation Criteria Overview

### Primary Requirements
- **Tailor resume to specific job** using relevant keywords and employer-aligned language
- **Highlight experience, achievements, and transferable skills**
- **Follow formatting best practices**
- **Use AI tools effectively while maintaining authenticity**

## Required Deliverables

### 1. Job Description
- Copy/paste or screenshot of a role you would like to apply to
- Must include clear requirements and qualifications
- Should contain specific skills and experience expectations

### 2. Tailored Resume
- **Format**: PDF or .docx
- **Content Requirements**:
  - Clear headline or summary statement
  - Highlighted achievements and responsibilities aligned with job description
  - Keywords from job description integrated naturally
  - Clean, professional formatting
  - Action-oriented bullet points (quantified where possible)

## Industry Best Practices

### ATS Optimization
- **ATS-friendly resume** that uses appropriate formatting
- No graphics, tables, or columns that confuse parsing
- Highlight relevant skills, achievements, and experiences
- Use quantifiable results and action-driven language

### Structure and Clarity
- **Best practices for structure, clarity, and keyword optimization**
- **Layout consistency** with clear headings
- **Action verbs** throughout the resume
- **Job-relevant keywords** naturally integrated

### AI Enhancement
- **Use AI tools to enhance resume content** while maintaining authentic personal voice
- **Humanized, authentic, and professional** final version
- **Strategic thinking** demonstrated through alignment choices

## Project Enhancement Suggestions

### Contextual Explanation
Include a brief explanation (2-3 sentences) of:
- **Why you selected this specific job description**
- **How your tailored resume addresses key requirements** in the role
- **Strategic alignment** between your skills and employer needs

### Additional Value-Add Elements
- **Keyword matching analysis** showing alignment percentage
- **Achievement quantification** with measurable results
- **Transferable skills demonstration** for career changers
- **Industry-specific terminology** integration

## Quality Standards

### Content Quality
- **Authentic voice** maintained throughout
- **Professional tone** appropriate for target industry
- **Error-free writing** with proper grammar and spelling
- **Consistent formatting** and style

### Technical Requirements
- **File compatibility** (PDF/.docx)
- **ATS readability** confirmed through testing
- **Mobile-friendly** formatting considerations
- **Print-ready** layout optimization

## Success Metrics

### Alignment Indicators
- **Keyword density**: 15-25% relevant keywords naturally integrated
- **Achievement focus**: 70%+ bullet points quantified
- **Action language**: 90%+ bullet points start with action verbs
- **Relevance score**: 85%+ alignment with job requirements

### Professional Standards
- **Readability score**: 8th-10th grade reading level
- **Length**: 1-2 pages maximum
- **Formatting consistency**: Uniform style throughout
- **Contact information**: Complete and professional
